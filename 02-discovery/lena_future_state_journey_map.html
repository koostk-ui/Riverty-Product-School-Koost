Lena's future state with the Riverty card

Strategy: make Riverty the trusted card heavy BNPL users carry every day, by handling returns and spending control better than anyone, without competing with our merchants.

Lena, the Decide-First Juggler. A salaried, phone-first online shopper who wants to receive and inspect what she bought before money leaves her account, and to see what she owes and when.

How Lena feels Cautious Relaxed In control Calm and organised

1Join and trust

User action

Adds card to Apple Pay, sees issuer, fees, SCHUFA impact → trusts it first

Internal state

Cautious about a new brand → clear answers turn doubt into confidence

Pain point addressed

Unknown issuer and SCHUFA fear → open answers before any bank link

2Shop anywhere

User action

Taps card online, picks pay-later per purchase → Riverty is always available

Internal state

Relaxed, no hunting for options → stops switching providers

Pain point addressed

Riverty missing at checkout → no fallback to Klarna, PayPal, or debit

3Keep or return

User action

Starts a return in the app → payment pauses until the merchant confirms

Internal state

In control, no chasing refunds → orders with confidence

Pain point addressed

Unclear return timing and refund waits → invoice updates right away

4See and settle

User action

Checks one view of dues, autopays small amounts → never misses dates

Internal state

Calm and organised → Riverty becomes her routine, not a fallback

Pain point addressed

Payments scattered across apps → one overview, reminders, no reminder fees

Three advantages over today's manual workaround

One place for dues and returnsReplaces scattered apps, notes and reminders.
"Decide first" works everywhere she shopsNo switching providers, or paying upfront and waiting.
Rewards route back to partner merchantsA reason to stay without competing with merchants.

Online first: in-store tap-to-pay would follow in a later phase, in line with the risk team's proposal to avoid early in-store declines.

Preconditions to confirm: Risk and Legal must confirm the real SCHUFA impact, and the return pause in stage 3 depends on card-return handling that is not yet scoped.

The third advantage is a hypothesis: merchant-funded rewards and the merchant benefit are untested.
